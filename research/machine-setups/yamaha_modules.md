# Yamaha RM50, RY30, CS6x and EX5: how the drum sounds are organised, kits, patterns, recordings and pack contents

Scope note: all four machines are covered under each question. Key numbers use Yamaha's note naming (C3 = MIDI 60, so C1 = 36), which is also Ableton's default. Machine-readable version: `~/research_notes/Vintage drum machine setups/yamaha_modules.json`.

## 1. Wave/sound categories, counts, number ranges and the official ordered lists

### Takeaway
All four have official lists in Yamaha's own manuals and data lists, and every list below is copied from them. All are numbered except the RY30 wave list, and the RM50 manual's misprinted row 30 is filled from an editor's source code. The RM50 has 133 ROM waveforms in ten groups. On top of these sit 500 preset voices in six numbered banks, and the "Yamaha RM 50" pack is numbered by those voice banks, not by the 133 waves. The RY30 has 90 waves (plus 84 reversed copies) and 96 voices. The CS6x has 145 drum and percussion waves (numbers 335-479) out of 479. The EX5 has 126 drum and percussion AWM waves (294-419), but its drum kits play numbered samples directly.

### Inferences
- RM50: the six voice banks, not the 133 waves, are what a "kit sound" means on this unit, so they are the right key for naming the pack's numbered files (see Q4).
- The RM50 ROM contains the whole RY30 wave set. 88 of the 90 RY30 wave names appear word for word in the RM50 list, and the other two are the timbales (RY30 TimbalH/L, RM50 TimblHi/Lo). The RM50 adds 43 more: BDDryT3, BDJazHi/Lo, SDDryT4, SDWdRim, SDDrMtl, SDDry5H/5S, SDFab, SDBshTp/SDBshSw (brush), CYCrsh2, CYCup2, TMJazz, TimCasc, 5 ambience waves (BDAmb, SDAmb, SideAmb, HatAmb, TomAmb) and 23 extra effects (Metal 1 ... MouthS2). This was found by comparing the two manuals' lists, and fits reviews calling the RM50 the RY30's rack companion.
- CS6x and EX5 drum waves are near-identical families (same names: BdStd, SdGM, T8/T9 sets, AnvlHt etc.); the CS6x adds BdVar1-5, SdVar1-2, SdAmb1-3, SdRoll, SdSwep, extra hats/cymbals and 'Bd Kit'/'Sn Kit' (inferred from the two lists).

### Gaps
- The RY30 manual prints waves without numbers, so the 1-90 positions used here are list order, not confirmed internal wave numbers.
- The full EX5 wave-ROM total (all categories) was not captured; only the drum/percussion block 294-419 was extracted.

## 2. Factory drum kit key map / default MIDI note map (key order)

### Takeaway
The CS6x and EX5 data lists print complete key maps for every factory kit, and both follow the GM/XG layout: kick C1 (36), snare D1 (38), closed hat F#1 (42), pedal hat G#1 (44), open hat A#1 (46), crash C#2 (49), ride D#2 (51). The RY30 manual prints all 12 factory pad banks (12 pads each) but no MIDI note defaults. The RM50 manual lists only kit names, with no per-key maps.

## 3. Sounds that cut each other off, per-sound controls, and effects

### Takeaway
All four have Yamaha "alternate groups": the RM50 and RY30 have 7, the CS6x and EX5 have 127. On the RY30 the factory hi-hat voices are already grouped (groups 1-3). Neither the RM50 nor the RY30 has any audio effects. On the CS6x and EX5, each drum key has its own reverb and chorus send and an insertion-effect switch, so drums go through the effects.

### Cited Findings
- **RM50:** per voice: Assign (mono, poly, mono/alt, poly/alt), Alternate group (off, 1-7), Output (stereo or ind1-ind6) and individual level 0-63. Mono/alt and poly/alt play the voice's two elements alternately, e.g. for snare rolls. The manual's example is putting open and closed hi-hat in one group. — [RM50 manual part 2 p.82](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RM50_Manual2.pdf)
- RM50 per-element parameters: wave, level, pan, pitch (+/-3 octaves, 1-cent steps), ADR amplitude EG with Punch, LPF12/LPF24/HPF12/HPF24 with cutoff 0-22.4 kHz and resonance, filter EG, pitch EG, LFO (triangle/saw/square/sine/S&H to amp, pitch or filter), Delay (1-7 repeats, 10 ms steps up to 1,280 ms, level and pitch offsets), 12 velocity curves and velocity sensitivities. Easy Edit on variations covers volume, pitch, decay, cutoff and element balance. — [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/2429); [Sound On Sound](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/10494); [rm50_manager parameter labels](https://github.com/draekko/rm50_manager)
- RM50 effects: none. "No on-board signal processor"; reverb sounds come from sampled ambience. — [Sound On Sound](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/10494); [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/2429)
- RM50 I/O: stereo out plus 6 individual outs, 6 trigger inputs, 3 waveform card slots and 1 data card slot. — [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/2429)
- **RY30:** alternate group (OFF, 1-7) per voice. The factory voice list puts Hat Cls1/Qtr1/Hlf1/Opn1/Ped1 (50-54) in group 1, Hat Cls2/Opn2 (55-56) in group 2 and analog Hat Cls3/Opn3 (57-58) in group 3; every other voice is OFF. The manual suggests open/muted triangle as another use. — [RY30 manual pp.89-90, 94](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RY30_Manual.pdf)
- RY30 per-voice parameters: level, wave assign and wave level (2 layers), pitch, decay, filter (LPF/HPF 12/24 dB, cutoff, resonance, filter EG), pitch EG, poly/mono, alternate group, output assign and individual level. Touch sensitivity is per voice for level, pitch, EG and filter. The real-time wheel controls pitch, decay, pan, filter and balance. — [RY30 manual pp.82-95 (Voice Edit pages 01-16)](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RY30_Manual.pdf); [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-ry30/836)
- RY30 effects: none ("no separate effects section"; VSE: "None, except that internal samples can be played in reverse"). — [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-ry30/836); [Vintage Synth Explorer](https://www.vintagesynth.com/yamaha/ry30)
- RY30 outputs, conflicting: MT says stereo L/R plus two individual outs, SOS says "2 pairs of stereo audio outputs", VSE says "four outputs". — [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-ry30/836); [Sound On Sound](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-ry30-rhythm-programmer/7445); [VSE](https://www.vintagesynth.com/yamaha/ry30)
- **CS6x:** per drum key: Element on/off, Wave Type (wave or normal voice), Receive Note Off, Assign Mode (single/multi), **Alternate Group (0 off, 1-127)**, Wave Number, Pan, Random Pan, Alternate Pan, Reverb Send, Chorus Send, Insertion Effect Switch (thru/ins1/ins2), Output Select, Level and level velocity, AEG attack/decay1/decay2/decay1 level, coarse tune +/-48, fine tune, pitch velocity, LPF cutoff and more. — [CS6x Data List p.36 (Drum Voice Key table)](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_CS6x_CS6r_DataList.pdf)
- CS6x effects: reverb (12 types), chorus (23), Insertion 1 (24) and Insertion 2 (92), plus a 4-band master EQ. Insert effects can be enabled per element. — [Sound On Sound Jan 2000](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/yamaha-cs6x); VSE gives 93 insertion types [VSE](https://www.vintagesynth.com/yamaha/cs6x)
- **EX5:** per drum key/layer: sample bank and number, coarse/fine tune, pitch EG, start offset, loop offset, key-on delay, sample play (fwd/rev), Receive Note Off, level, pan/random pan, Reverb Send, Chorus Send, level velocity, Output Select, **InsEF Elem Sw (1/2/off)**, velocity low/high/crossfade, **Alternate Group (off, 1-127)**, Key Assign (single/multi). The manual's example is closed and open hi-hat in one group. — [EX5 manual pp.143-146](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_EX5_EX7_Manual.pdf)
- EX5 effects: reverb (12), chorus (17) and two insertion processors (24 and 79). In Performance mode, insert effects are limited to 4 AWM parts, or 1 if VL/AN/FDSP voices are used. — [Sound On Sound May 1998](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/yamaha-ex5); [EX5 manual p.39](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_EX5_EX7_Manual.pdf)

### Inferences
- In an Ableton Drum Rack, the RY30 and RM50 hi-hat groups map to choke groups (RY30 factory: group 1 = 50-54, group 2 = 55-56, group 3 = 57-58). For the CS6x and EX5 kits, putting F#1/G#1/A#1 in one choke group copies the usual Yamaha setup, but the factory value is not published (inferred).

### Gaps
- Factory alternate-group settings inside the CS6x and EX5 preset kits, and for the RM50 preset voices, are not printed in the sources found.

## 4. What each pack folder contains, and whether its numbering matches ROM order

## 6. Famous recordings that verifiably used these machines

### Takeaway
None verified for any of the four. Hardware-to-record evidence for these late Yamaha units is thin, and the web-search budget for this task ran out before a dedicated search could be done.

### Cited Findings
- Vintage Synth Explorer lists Faithless and Depeche Mode as CS6x users, without naming tracks. — [VSE CS6x](https://www.vintagesynth.com/yamaha/cs6x)
- The RY30 Dance & Soul and House & Rap cards were programmed by Gota (ex-Melon/Major Force), who worked with Soul II Soul, Bomb the Bass and Massive Attack. The article does not say any of them used an RY30 on record. — [Music Technology](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-ry30/836)
- None of these name artists or recordings: SOS RM50, MT RM50, SOS/MT RY30, SOS EX5, VSE RY30/EX5, Wikipedia EX5. — [SOS RM50](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/10494); [MT RM50](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rm50/2429); [SOS EX5](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/yamaha-ex5); [Wikipedia EX5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_EX5)

### Gaps
- No verified track (artist, title, year, evidence) and no published groove transcription was found for any machine. The famous_tracks fields are empty rather than guessed.
