# Yamaha RX5, RX11, RX21, RX21L, MR-10 and TG33: hardware setup, presets and famous uses (for Ableton Drum Rack + MIDI clips)

Method note (applies to every section): the owner's manuals for RX5, RX11, RX21, RX21L and TG33 are image-only scans hosted by soundprogramming.net (each model page links its manual; the PDFs only load with that page as referrer). I read them by rendering pages in memory and running OCR. Nothing was saved to disk. "PDF p.N" means the Nth page of that scan. OCR is reliable for running text. Where a table was partly garbled, or a value had to be read off a drawing (MIDI keyboard diagrams), I flag it under Inferences. Web search ran out partway through (shared budget), which mostly limited the famous-track and pattern-chart searches.

## 1. What voices does each machine have, in front-panel/pad order, with exact labels?

### Takeaway
All four RX units have full panel/voice charts in their manuals. The RX5 has 24 lettered keys (A–L top, M–X bottom). The RX11 has 16 buttons in 2 rows of 8. The RX21 has 9 buttons in one row. The RX21L has the same 9 buttons, but 7 of them hold two sounds (16 total). The MR-10 has only 5 playable pads (BD, SD, high tom, low tom, cymbal). The TG33 has no pads: its drum kit is preset voice "DR:Kit", which maps 61 sounds from C1 to C6.

### Cited Findings
#### RX5 (24 instrument keys)
- The keys are lettered A–L (top row) and M–X (bottom row). The manual's chart "Instrument keys and internal voices" gives the factory layout. Top row, left to right: A BASS DRUM 2, B SNARE DRUM 2, C RIMSHOT 2, D ELECTRIC TOM 1, E ELECTRIC TOM 2, F ELECTRIC TOM 3, G ELECTRIC TOM 4, H HI HAT (OPEN), I RIDE (EDGE), J CHINESE CYMBAL, K TAMBOURINE, L SHAKER. Bottom row: M BASS DRUM 1, N SNARE DRUM 1, O RIMSHOT 1, P TOM 1, Q TOM 2, R TOM 3, S TOM 4, T HI-HAT (CLOSED), U RIDE (CUP), V CRASH, W CLAPS, X COWBELL — [RX5 owner's manual, PDF p.9 / printed p.5](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf) (linked from [soundprogramming RX5 page](https://soundprogramming.net/drum-machines/yamaha/yamaha-rx5/))
- The LCD uses abbreviated names (BD1, SD 1, RIM 1, E.Tom 1, Tom 1, HH clos, HH open, Edge, Cup, China, Crash, Tambrn, Claps, Shaker, Cowbel), with prefixes "Int-" (internal), "Cp" (copied/RAM) and "Crt-" (cartridge) — [RX5 manual, PDF p.12 / printed p.8](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- The panel spec lists "INSTRUMENTS A THRU X (24)", ACCENT 1 and ACCENT 2 buttons, 12 INSTRUMENT VOLUME sliders, and REVERSE and DAMP keys — [RX5 manual, PDF p.74 / printed p.70](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- Sound On Sound (April 1987, David Mellor) gives the same 24 internal voices: BD 1–2, SD 1–2, Rimshot 1–2, Toms 1–4, Electric toms 1–4, HH open, HH closed, ride edge, ride bell, Chinese, crash, tambourine, claps, shaker, cowbell — [SOS review on mu:zines](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx5/1956)

#### RX11 (16 black instrument buttons, 2 rows of 8)
- The manual's button illustration shows the top row as SD1, TOM1, TOM2, BD1, HH OPEN, RIDE, COWBELL, SHAKER, and the bottom row as SD2, TOM3, TOM4, BD2, HH CLOSED, CRASH, RIMSHOT, CLAPS. Multi-function buttons are starred — [RX11 owner's manual, PDF p.7](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf) (linked from [soundprogramming RX11 page](https://soundprogramming.net/drum-machines/yamaha/yamaha-rx11))
- Instrument chart: SD1 and SD2 each offer Heavy snare, Medium snare, Light snare and Hi-tune snare 1–5. TOM1 is a 10" deep-body tom, TOM2 a 12" deep-body tom, TOM3 a 14" floor tom, and TOM4 another floor tom (its size is illegible in the scan). BD1 and BD2 each offer Heavy, Medium 1 and Medium 2 bass drum. HH OPEN offers Open Hi-Hat 1/2. HH CLOSED offers Closed Hi-Hat 1/2 and Pedalled Hi-Hat. RIDE and CRASH each have one cymbal. COWBELL offers Cowbell 1/2, RIMSHOT offers Rimshot 1/2, and CLAPS offers Hand claps 1/2 — [RX11 manual, PDF p.7](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)
- To change which voice a multi-function button plays: hold FUNCTION, press INST-CHANGE, press the button, then step through with +1/YES and −1/NO. Only SD1, SD2, BD1, BD2, HH OPEN, HH CLOSED, COWBELL, RIMSHOT and CLAPS respond — [RX11 manual, PDF p.7](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)
- Conflict: the March 1984 E&MM preview (pre-release) describes an "RX11 with 16 sounds". - The instrument chart lists SD (medium-tuned snare), TOM 1 (10" deep-body tom-tom), TOM 2 (12" deep-body), TOM 3 (14" floor tom-tom), BD (bass drum), HH OPEN, HH CLOSED, CYM (crash cymbal) and CLAPS (hand claps) — [RX21 owner's manual, PDF p.8](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf); text version on [manualsdir p.9](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/228683/yamaha-rx21.html?page=9)

- Instrument chart: BONGO H (high bongo), BONGO L, TIMBALE H, TIMBALE L, AGOGO H, AGOGO L, COWBELL, CLAVES, CONGA H MUTE (muted high conga), CONGA H OPEN (open high conga), CONGA L, TAMBOURINE, CUICA H, CUICA L, WHISTLE 1 (short whistle), WHISTLE 2 (long whistle) — [RX21L manual, PDF p.8](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)

Paired buttons switch together (e.g. BONGO HI and BONGO LO both change to the timbales) — [RX21L manual, PDF p.9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)
- E&MM (June 1986, Trevor Gilchrist) calls it "basically an RX21 with 16 latin voices in place of the nine traditional 'kit' sounds" — [E&MM review](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx21l-drum-machine/1900)

#### MR-10 (5 finger pads)
- The pads are Bass Drum, Snare Drum, High Tom, Low Tom and Cymbal. The controls are a preset selector, tempo, "BD VOLUME", "CY VOLUME" and "TUNE" — [nicole.express teardown, Jan 2026](https://nicole.express/2026/yamaha-is-mr-ten.html); [Samples From Mars](https://samplesfrommars.com/products/mr10-from-mars) also names 5 pads: Bass Drum, Snare Drum, Hi Tom, Low Tom, Cymbal

Sequencer.de lists "7 voices" — [sequencer.de](https://www.sequencer.de/syns/yamaha/MR10.html)
- Samples From Mars sampled closed and open hats "found within the loops" (the preset rhythms), each at 6 pitches — [Samples From Mars](https://samplesfrommars.com/products/mr10-from-mars)

#### TG33 (module, no pads)
- "Voice number P87 provides a complete drum kit plus a range of valuable percussion sounds… each note produces a different drum sound." The table is headed "Voice Number P1 87 DR:Kit: Drum-set Voice". The full key map is in section 5 — [TG33 operating manual part 1, PDF p.33 / printed p.27](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_TG33_Manual1.pdf) (linked from [soundprogramming TG33 page](https://soundprogramming.net/synthesizers/yamaha/yamaha-tg33))
- The preset voice list includes 8.4 SQ*Wrapa ("Rap percussion"), 8.5 SQ*TG809 ("Analog drum machine. Lower octaves for kicks and toms. Upper octaves for snares and hand claps."), 8.6 SQ*Devol ("Rap drums"), 8.7 DR:Kit ("A fairly orthodox drum and percussion kit", 2 elements, wave 127 "Drum set", effect Rev Plate) and 8.8 DR*EFX ("90's phased drums. Play hard for full phase effect.", 4 elements, effect Dly&Rev 2) — [TG33 manual part 1, PDF p.29 / printed p.23](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_TG33_Manual1.pdf); synthmania describes "87 DR-Kit… punchy kit, plus a few vocal sample surprises" and "88 DR-EFX… filtered, effected drum kit" — [synthmania TG33](https://synthmania.com/tg33.htm)

### Inferences
- RX5: when the request says "left to right", the faithful 24-pad Drum Rack order is A–L then M–X. Alternatively, keep the two-row physical layout as two pad rows: top row = the "2"/electric/alternate voices, bottom row = the "1"/acoustic voices.
- MR-10: hats, brush and shaker exist only inside the preset rhythms and have no pad. That conclusion comes from the SFM sampling notes and the teardown's 7-instrument list. Exact panel silkscreen spellings are not confirmed.
- TG33: there is no front panel order. Key order C1–C6 is the only "official" layout.

### Gaps
- Exact MR-10 silkscreen labels and the full preset-rhythm instrument table: the MR-10 manual mirrors were unreachable (manualslib and manualowl returned 403, oldschoolsound5.free.fr no longer resolves).
- RX11 TOM4 drum size is illegible in the scan.

## 2. Which sounds share one voice or cut each other off?

### Takeaway
The RX5 is the clearest case: each vertical key pair (A/M … L/X) is one output channel that plays one sound at a time. That gives 12 choke pairs, including open/closed hat. The RX11 has four "not on the same beat" groups. The RX21 has HH open/closed. The RX21L has three pairs. Nothing is documented for the MR-10 or the TG33 DR:Kit.

### Cited Findings
- RX5: "Up to 12 sounds may be played simultaneously… if you press two instrument keys in the same pair (for example, A and M, or L and X) you will only hear one sound, because only one sound can be output from each of the RX5's 12 output channels." The pairs are BD2/BD1, SD2/SD1, RIM2/RIM1, E.TOM1/TOM1, E.TOM2/TOM2, E.TOM3/TOM3, E.TOM4/TOM4, HH OPEN/HH CLOSED, RIDE EDGE/RIDE CUP, CHINESE/CRASH, TAMBOURINE/CLAPS and SHAKER/COWBELL — [RX5 manual, PDF p.10 & p.9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- Polyphony conflict: Polynominal says "16 digital voices polyphony" and Vintage Synth Explorer and current Wikipedia also say 16. The manual says 12 simultaneous sounds — [polynominal](https://www.polynominal.com/m/yamaha-rx-5.htm); [vintagesynth](https://vintagesynth.com/yamaha/rx5); [Wikipedia RX-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_RX-5)
- RX11: "Instruments which can not be used on the same beat are: RIMSHOT, SD1 and SD2; TOM3 and TOM4; BD1 and BD2; HH OPEN and HH CLOSED" — [RX11 manual, PDF p.8](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf). E&MM notes "The toms can sound together on the same beat" — [E&MM Oct 1984](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx11-and-rx15/1465?theme=1)
- RX21: HH OPEN and HH CLOSED "cannot be used on the same beat… programming in an HH OPEN note on the same beat will clear the HH CLOSED note and replace it… and vice versa" — [RX21 manual, PDF p.8](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf)
- RX21L: "The following instrument pairs will not produce sound together (on the same beat). Even if you press both buttons simultaneously or trigger both at the same time using MIDI note numbers… only one will sound. CUICA HI/CUICA LO, WHISTLE 1/WHISTLE 2, CONGA HI MUTE/CONGA HI OPEN." The button pairs (BONGO H/TIMBALE H etc.) are switched alternates, not simultaneous voices — [RX21L manual, PDF p.9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)

### Inferences
- In a Drum Rack, make each RX5 key pair a choke group (12 groups). This copies the one-voice-per-channel hardware.
- RX11: the choke groups line up with the shared individual outputs (SD/RM, BD and HH outs). That suggests one voice channel per group, so choke groups for {RIMSHOT, SD1, SD2}, {TOM3, TOM4}, {BD1, BD2} and {HH OPEN, HH CLOSED} are reasonable (inferred).
- RX21/RX21L: the manuals only say "not on the same beat". Whether a closed hat cuts off a ringing open hat on a later step isn't stated. A choke group is still the conventional choice (inferred).

### Gaps
- MR-10 open/closed hat behaviour inside presets: unknown.
- TG33 DR:Kit: no exclusive/alternate group is documented in the TG33 manual (unknown). The TG33 has no drum-edit mode in the manual's index.

## 3. What controls does each voice have? How do accent/dynamics, flam, roll and tuning work?

### Takeaway
The RX5 is the deep one: per-voice pitch, a 6-stage envelope, pitch bend, level, loop, reverse and damp, plus two accent levels per key. The RX11 has per-instrument level, pan (15 steps) and accent level, but no tuning. The RX21/RX21L have only level and accent level, with fixed panning. The MR-10 has one TUNE knob, BD and cymbal volumes, and no accent. The TG33 kit is velocity-sensitive, with whole-voice editing only.

### Cited Findings
#### RX5
- Edit Voice jobs (per voice):
  - Job 02 Pitch: ±semitones, with 10-cent steps when "0" is held. Range +24/−36 semitones.
  - Job 03 Envelope: Attack Rate 01–99, Decay 1 Rate 01–99, Decay 1 Level 01–60, Decay 2 Rate 01–99, Release Rate 01–99, Gate Time 0.1–6.5 s.
  - Job 04 Bend: Rate 0–60 and Range ±60 semitones.
  - Job 05 Voice Level 0–31.
  - Job 06 Loop on/off.
  - — [RX5 manual, PDF pp.39–41 / printed pp.35–37](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- REVERSE key: plays any voice backwards, live or while writing. It uses the original (unedited) EG reversed and does not reverse pitch bend. DAMP: in real-time write, a second key press damps a ringing note, and it can also be driven by MIDI note-off — [RX5 manual, PDF p.10](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- Accents: "Pressing any instrument key while holding either of the green ACCENT keys causes that instrument to sound at its ACCENT 1 or ACCENT 2 level." Two accent levels are set per key (Key Assign Job 05), each an offset of ±31 on the 0–31 voice level, clipped at 31/0 — [RX5 manual, PDF p.10 & p.48](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- INSTRUMENT VOLUME faders set the level of each key pair. Key Assign Job 02 adds per-key offsets for pitch, level, attack and decay. Pattern edit can change 5 parameters per individual note — [RX5 manual, PDF p.10, p.13, p.46](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf); MT: "Up to five editable parameters per note", "Quantise to 1/48 or off" — [Music Technology Apr 1987](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx5/1993)
- The keys are not velocity sensitive — [vintagesynth](https://vintagesynth.com/yamaha/rx5). External MIDI velocity can scale envelope parameters (MIDI Job 06 "E.G. Velocity") — [RX5 manual, PDF p.69](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- Stereo position is fixed per output channel (15 = left, 1 = right, 8 = centre). Channels 1–12 are set to 8, 9, 9, 12, 10, 6, 4, 13, 3, 11, 5, 8 (ch1 = A/M BD … ch12 = L/X shaker/cowbell) — [RX5 manual, PDF p.9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)
- No flam or roll function is documented. Swing is 54/58/63/67/71% and only works with quantize 1/8 or 1/16 — [RX5 manual, PDF p.19](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf)

#### RX11
- INST LEVEL is 00–31 per instrument (nominal 15). PAN places each instrument at one of 15 positions, and only works in STEREO OUT mode. ACCENT LEVEL is 00–31 per instrument and is added to the instrument level (15 + 05 = 20). Holding ACCENT while striking an instrument plays it at its accent level, live or in either write mode — [RX11 manual, PDF pp.8–9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)
- Pan, instrument level and accent level are stored per song, and instrument and accent per pattern — [RX11 manual spec, PDF p.32](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)
- No per-voice tuning: E&MM criticises the "lack of user-tunability". Quantize runs from 1/4 to 1/192, and at 1/192 "flam effects in real time" are possible — [E&MM Oct 1984](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx11-and-rx15/1465?theme=1)
- Swing values are 50% (off), 54, 58, 63, 67 and 71%. It works only in real-time write with quantize 1/8 or 1/16. One OCR'd line says "75%", while the diagram and the list say 71% — [RX11 manual, PDF p.16](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)

#### RX21
- Total level is 00–63. Instrument level is 00–31 (start at 15). Accent level is 00–31 per instrument and is added to the instrument level, capped at 31, so an instrument at 31 cannot be accented — [RX21 manual, PDF pp.8–10](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf)
- In patterns, the accent is per beat: "pressing the ACCENT key to add an accent to a beat (no need to press an instrument key…) will affect ALL instruments sounding on that beat, according to their individual accent levels." Live, hold ACCENT and tap keys — [RX21 manual, PDF p.10](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf); H&SR: "you can't program the accent for individual voices" — [Home & Studio Recording Feb 1986](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/compromising-position/11498)
- Fixed panning: left of centre HH OPEN, HH CLOSED, TOM 1; centre SD, BD, TOM 2; right of centre CLAPS, CYM, TOM 3. Mono comes from the L jack — [manualsdir RX21 p.10](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/228683/yamaha-rx21.html?page=10); "position in the stereo is not [adjustable]" — [One Two Testing Oct 1985](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx21/11881?theme=1)
- There is no tuning, flam, roll or swing in the manual. Quantize values are 1/12, 1/16, 1/24 and 1/32. Patterns are one bar of 1–16 sixteenths — [RX21 manual, PDF p.13](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf)

#### RX21L
- Levels and accent levels work exactly as on the RX21 (00–31, accent added to level) — [RX21L manual, PDF p.11](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)
- Fixed pan values (0 = far right, 15 = far left): BONGO H 6, BONGO L 4, TIMBALE H 10, TIMBALE L 6, AGOGO H 5, AGOGO L 3, COWBELL 8, CLAVES 11, CONGA H MUTE 12, CONGA H OPEN 12, CONGA L 10, TAMBOURINE 13, CUICA H 9, CUICA L 7, WHISTLE 1 3, WHISTLE 2 13 — [RX21L manual, PDF p.11](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)
- Quantize values are 12, 24, 16 and 32. Pattern lengths are 01–16 sixteenths — [RX21L manual, PDF p.15](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf)

#### MR-10
- Only the bass drum and cymbal have their own volume knobs. A master tune control adjusts the snare and toms — [Yamaha Black Boxes](https://yamahablackboxes.com/collection/yamaha-mr10-drum-machine/). Conflict: the teardown says TUNE affects "only the high and low toms" — [nicole.express](https://nicole.express/2026/yamaha-is-mr-ten.html). A sale listing says Tune "adjusts the toms and cymbal" — [matrixsynth 2024 listing](https://www.matrixsynth.com/2024/10/yamaha-mr-10-vintage-analog-drum.html)
- The bass drum can also be triggered by an optional pedal, and pad and pedal "can be used simultaneously". There is a single mixed output — [nicole.express](https://nicole.express/2026/yamaha-is-mr-ten.html); [Yamaha Black Boxes](https://yamahablackboxes.com/collection/yamaha-mr10-drum-machine/)
- All sounds come from IC7 (YM1023), with only "basic filters" outside the chip — [nicole.express](https://nicole.express/2026/yamaha-is-mr-ten.html)

#### TG33
- Voice-wide controls only. Effect type, balance and send. Pitch-bend range 0–12. Wheel and aftertouch routing to AM/PM/pitch/level. Voice envelope AR/RR offsets. Per-element wave, volume, pan, velocity sensitivity and LFO — [TG33 manual part 2, PDF pp.5–9 / printed pp.45–49](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_TG33_Manual2.PDF)

### Inferences
- RX5 to Drum Rack: per-pad Simpler with transpose, ADSR-like envelope, pitch envelope (bend) and a reverse toggle is faithful. Map ACCENT 1/2 to two velocity tiers, and pan each pad to its channel's fixed position, not freely.
- RX21 accent is a whole-step accent. In MIDI clips, an "X" step should raise velocity on every voice hit on that step.
- TG33 DR:Kit has 2 elements. With 32-note polyphony that gives about 16 simultaneous drum notes (inferred from the element count and "32-note" spec).

### Gaps
- MR-10 accent: none documented (unknown).
- RX5 Key Assign "Multi Step" exact parameters weren't read in full.

## 4. Built-in effects?

### Takeaway
None on any of the RX units or the MR-10. RX5 reverse, damp and bend are voice functions, not effects. The TG33 has 16 digital effects, and they apply only to its OUTPUT 1.

### Cited Findings
- None of the RX5, RX11, RX21 or RX21L manuals or reviews describe a reverb, delay or filter. The RX5 is described as having "no filter" and an LFO limited to pitch "BEND" — [polynominal](https://www.polynominal.com/m/yamaha-rx-5.htm); [vintagesynth](https://vintagesynth.com/yamaha/rx5)
- MR-10: only filters inside the sound circuit, no effects — [nicole.express](https://nicole.express/2026/yamaha-is-mr-ten.html)
- TG33 effect types: Rev Hall, Rev Room, Rev Plate, Rev Club, Rev Metal, Delay 1 (short single), Delay 2 (long), Delay 3 (long), Doubler, Ping-Pong, Pan Ref (panned reflections), Early Ref, Gate Rev, Dly&Rev 1, Dly&Rev 2, Dist&Rev. Each has balance 0–127 and send 0–127 — [TG33 manual part 2, PDF p.5 / printed p.45](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_TG33_Manual2.PDF)
- "effects will only apply to OUTPUT 1." There are 2 stereo output pairs, and Multi mode assigns voice groups to them — [TG33 manual part 2, PDF pp.40–41 / printed pp.80–81](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/Yamaha_TG33_Manual2.PDF)

### Inferences
- For a faithful TG33 DR:Kit, add a plate reverb (the voice's stored effect is "Rev Plate"). Effect depth is unknown.

### Gaps
- Stored balance and send values for the DR:Kit effect: unknown.

## 5. Total sounds, official ordered lists (TG33 key map) and default MIDI note maps

### Takeaway
The manuals give firm default note maps for the RX5 and RX11, a stated note range plus a keyboard diagram for the RX21/RX21L, and a 61-key DR:Kit map for the TG33. All of them use Yamaha octave naming (C3 = middle C = 60). The MR-10 has no MIDI.

- RX21 per-key map, read off the manual's keyboard drawing (medium confidence): BD 45, TOM3 48, TOM2 50, SD 52, TOM1 53, CLAPS 55, HH CLOSED 57, HH OPEN 59, CYM 60.

  - The endpoints match the stated range (45–60).
  - It mirrors the RX11 map except CLAPS (RX11: 54; RX21 diagram: 55).
  - The manual's note names are inconsistent: "45 (A2)" vs "60 (C3)" with C3 = 60 makes 45 = A1.
- RX21L per-key map from the drawing (C–B, two octaves; range 64–79 stated). Medium-high confidence, because all 16 consecutive keys 64–79 are used and the low/high pairs sit next to each other: 64 CONGA L, 65 CONGA H OPEN, 66 CONGA H MUTE, 67 BONGO L, 68 BONGO H, 69 TIMBALE L, 70 TIMBALE H, 71 TAMBOURINE, 72 COWBELL, 73 CLAVES, 74 AGOGO L, 75 AGOGO H, 76 CUICA L, 77 CUICA H, 78 WHISTLE 1, 79 WHISTLE 2.

- Four TG33 table cells were partly garbled in OCR: A2 "HH closed", G2 "Cowbell" (possibly "Cowbell 1", since A#4 is "Cowbell 2"), B3 "Tambourine" and B4 "Crash". These were reconstructed from two OCR passes. B4 "Crash" is probably the SFX-category "Crash" wave (AWM 69), not a cymbal (inferred).

- Official ROM order for RX5/RX11 internal voices is not published. The lists above are in key order and manual chart order.
- RX5 cartridge third-row column placement: unresolved.

## 6. What does each pack folder contain?

### Takeaway
All folders hold one-shot 16-bit/44.1 kHz WAVs with no loops. There are two styles. The hyphenated folders ("Yamaha RX-5", "Yamaha RX-21", "Yamaha TG-33") are stereo files with plain-English names. The un-hyphenated folders ("Yamaha RX5", "RX11", "RX21", "RX21L", "MR10") are mono files with a "RX5Kick01"-style prefix. Only the RX21, RX-21 and RX21L folders are complete kits. (Facts in this section come from a read-only `ls -R` and `afinfo` of ~/Splice/sounds/packs; no files were changed or played.)

### Cited Findings
- **Yamaha RX-5**: 13 stereo files. Bassdrum, Bassdrum-02, Snaredrum, Snaredrum-02, Snaredrum-03, Rimshot, Tom (one only), Hat Closed, Hat Open, Cowbell, Shaker, Tambourine, SFX. Durations are 0.09–1.0 s. No claps, ride, crash or china. (local listing)
- **Yamaha RX5**: 28 mono files.
  - Kicks and snares: RX5Kick01–06, RX5Snare01–07.
  - Kit pieces: RX5Rimshot, RX5Clap, RX5Hat_C, RX5Hat_O, RX5Crash, RX5China, RX5Cup, RX5Edge, RX5Tamborine (sic).
  - Cartridge voices: RX5Bongo_Hi/Lo, RX5Timbale_Hi/Lo.
  - Unofficial names: RX5Smack, RX5Thud.
  - No toms, E-toms, cowbell or shaker. (local listing)
- **Yamaha RX11**: 10 mono files. RX11Kick, RX11Snare01/02, RX11Rimshot, RX11Clap, RX11Hat_C, RX11Hat_O, RX11Hat_P (pedal), RX11Ride, RX11Crash (2.76 s). No toms, cowbell or shaker. (local listing)
- **Yamaha RX-21**: 9 stereo files: Bassdrum, Snaredrum, Tom H, Tom M, Tom L, Closed Hat, Open hat, Crash, Clap. **Yamaha RX21**: 9 mono files: RX21Kick, RX21Snare, RX21Tom_Hi/Mid/Lo, RX21Hat_C, RX21Hat_O, RX21Crash, RX21Clap. Both are complete 9-voice sets, but durations differ (e.g. closed hat 0.26 s vs 0.10 s). (local listing)
- **Yamaha RX21L**: 16 mono files, a complete set: RX21LAgogoHi/Lo, BongoHi/Lo, Claves, CongaHi, CongaLo, CongaM, Cowbell, CuicaHi/Lo, Tambourine, TimbaleHi/Lo, WhistleL, WhistleS. (local listing)
- **Yamaha MR10**: 11 mono files: MR10Kick01, Kick02, Snare, Tom_Hi, Tom_Mid, Tom_Lo, Cymbal, Hat_C, Hat_O, Brush, Shaker. (local listing)
- **Yamaha TG-33**: 51 stereo files:
  - Bassdrum-01–04, Snaredrum-01–05, Tom-01–04 and Tom-06–07 (no Tom-05).
  - Bongo-01/02, Clap, Clave, Conga, Cowbell, Cowbell H, Cowbell L, Crash-01/02, Crash Reverse, Ride-01/02, Hat Open (no closed hat).
  - Rimshot, Shaker, Snap, Tambourine, Timbale, Triangle-01/02, Whistle-01/02, Woodblock (0.01 s).
  - Glass-01/02, Flute, SFX-01–08, Unknown.
  - Many files are exactly 0.52 s long. (local listing)

### Inferences
- RX21 toms: Hi = TOM 1 (10"), Mid = TOM 2 (12"), Lo = TOM 3 (14"), per the manual sizes.
- RX21L names: CongaM = CONGA H MUTE, CongaHi = CONGA H OPEN, WhistleS = WHISTLE 1 (short), WhistleL = WHISTLE 2 (long).
- RX5 folders: there are 6 kicks and 7 snares, but the hardware has only BD1/BD2/BD3 and SD1/SD2/SD3 (internal plus cartridge). The extras are probably edited, pitched or enveloped variants or other cartridges (unverifiable). Bongo and Timbale come from the RX5 ROM cartridge.
- MR10: Tom_Mid is probably a TUNE-knob variant (there's no mid-tom pad). Hat_C, Hat_O, Brush and Shaker were probably taken from preset rhythms.
- TG-33: the pack goes beyond the DR:Kit (no bongos, woodblock or glass in the DR:Kit; Flute is AWM wave 16). Mapping to official DR:Kit names would be guesswork except the obvious ones (Snaredrum-01–05 ≈ SD 1–5, Triangle-01/02 ≈ Triangle closed/open, Snap ≈ Finger snaps, Crash Reverse ≈ Reverse Cymbal). The 0.52 s cap looks like a truncation choice by the pack maker.

### Gaps
- Which exact hardware voice each un-numbered or variant file comes from (RX5 Kick01–06, Snare01–07, Smack, Thud, SFX; TG-33 SFX-01–08, Unknown).

## 7. Factory preset patterns: names and step grids

### Takeaway
None of the five pattern-capable machines has published factory pattern grids that I could access. The RX5, RX11 and RX21L presets are numbered, not named. The RX21's 44 named presets live in a separate "RX21 Pattern Book" that I couldn't find. Only the MR-10's 12 preset names are documented. All grids are null.

### Cited Findings
- RX5: "a variety of factory-programmed Patterns in Pattern locations 00 thru 54. These include basic rock rhythms, complex fills, syn-drum type sequences, and interesting melodic Patterns." They can be reloaded by holding ACCENT 1 at power-on. Pattern 00 is two bars — [RX5 manual, PDF pp.13–14](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX5_Manual.pdf). There are three pre-programmed demo songs, including "Funky Up" — [SOS](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx5/1956); [Yamaha Black Boxes](https://yamahablackboxes.com/collection/yamaha-rx5-drum-machine/)
- RX11: "As shipped, the RX11 pattern memory contains 37 simple factory-programmed patterns—in pattern memories 00 through 36" — [RX11 manual, PDF p.9](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX11_Manual.pdf)
- RX21:
  - Patterns 56–99 are "permanent preset patterns". The 44 presets are "detailed in the accompanying RX21 PATTERN BOOK" — [RX21 manual, PDF p.13](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21_Manual.pdf); [manualsdir p.3](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/228683/yamaha-rx21.html?page=3)
  - One Two Testing lists the content as four blank bars of varying length, rock, disco, samba, fill-ins, bossa nova, basic reggae, and an "end" bar with "accented cymbal and bass drum" — [One Two Testing](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx21/11881?theme=1)
  - IM names "Pattern No. 52… Rock Shuffle", which conflicts with presets living at 56–99 — [IM](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx21/5105)
- RX21L: "the internal pattern memory contains 29 patterns and 2 songs pre-programmed by Yamaha". These sit in user RAM (00–49), and the manual advises saving them to tape — [RX21L manual, PDF p.15](https://soundprogramming.net/manuals/drum/Yamaha_RX21L_Manual.pdf); E&MM: "29 are factory presets" — [E&MM](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/yamaha-rx21l-drum-machine/1900)
- MR-10:
  - 12 presets: Disco, 16 Beat, Jazz Rock, Samba, Slow Rock, Mambo, Swing, Rhumba, Waltz, Bossa Nova, March, Tango. Three variations: Normal, 4-bar, 8-bar — [matrixsynth 2024 listing](https://www.matrixsynth.com/2024/10/yamaha-mr-10-vintage-analog-drum.html)
  - The manual's contents (search snippet only) list Disco, Jazz Rock, Slow Rock, Waltz, March, 16 Beat, Tango, Samba and Mambo under "Preset Rhythm Patterns" — [manualslib MR10](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/341316/Yamaha-Mr10.html)
  - Presets "you can combine… to make more complex rhythms" — [Audiofanzine user review](https://en.audiofanzine.com/drum-machine/yamaha/MR10/)
  - Patterns cannot be recorded — [Yamaha Black Boxes](https://yamahablackboxes.com/collection/yamaha-mr10-drum-machine/)
- TG33: no sequencer — [synthmania](https://synthmania.com/tg33.htm)

### Inferences
- The MR-10 manual section "Preset Rhythm Patterns" probably contains notation for each rhythm (inferred from its contents listing). That is the most promising source for real grids.

### Gaps
- No step grids found for any factory pattern. The RX21 Pattern Book and the MR-10 manual pages weren't reachable. A search on mu:zines or archive.org for "RX21 Pattern Book" and the MR-10 English manual is the next step. Search budget ran out before I could do it.
- Unverified claim (source unclear, search snippet only): "BD volume switches permit insertion of a rhythmic break every 4, 8 or 16 bars" (MR-10).

## 8. Famous recordings that verifiably used each machine (with grids)

### Takeaway
I could verify only two: the RX5 on The Sisters of Mercy's *Floodland* (written on it, and its kick/snare/hats resampled) and the RX11 in New Order's *Brotherhood* equipment list. Erasure's RX11 use is reported only in general terms. No published step charts were found for any of them.

### Cited Findings
- RX5: Andrew Eldritch "got a Yamaha RX5 for the snare sound (the kick was quite tight too) and wrote the album with that". Also: "Resampled RX5 kick, snare and hats formed the rest of the skeleton crew" (in an Akai S900) for *Floodland* (1987) — [The Sisters of Mercy official site, Doktor Avalanche tech page](http://www.the-sisters-of-mercy.com/tech/doktors.htm); "the Doktor was upgraded to a Yamaha RX5" — [Wikipedia: Doktor Avalanche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_Avalanche)
- RX5 user list in a 2016 Wikipedia revision (no citations): A-ha, Cameo, Cocteau Twins, Madonna, Pet Shop Boys, Prince, Vangelis, New Order, OMD (*Sugar Tax*), Kino (*Zvezda po imeni Solntse*), Stock Aitken Waterman ("You Spin Me Round"), etc. — [Wikipedia RX-5 (2016 mirror)](https://bafybeiemxf5abjwjbikoz4mc3a3dla6ual3jsgpdr4cjr3oz3evfyavhwq.ipfs.4everland.io/wiki/Yamaha_RX-5.html). The current article has no user list — [Wikipedia RX-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_RX-5)
- RX11: the equipment list for New Order's *Brotherhood* (1986) includes "Yamaha RX11 drum machine", alongside a TR808 and an Oberheim DMX. No song is named — [Sound On Sound Feb 1987, "State of the art"](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/state-of-the-art/1516)
- RX11: "Erasure's drum sound often came from the Yamaha RX-11" (no track named) — [Gearnews, 2022](https://www.gearnews.com/how-to-sound-like-vince-clarke-of-depeche-mode-yazoo-and-erasure/). A sample vendor says it was used by Erasure "for their first album's tour" and by Aphex Twin on an unnamed track on *Syro* — [SampleScience](https://www.samplescience.info/2020/09/rx-11-hd.html)
- RX11 Wikipedia users also include Кино, Los Prisioneros, Nik Kershaw and They Might Be Giants, with weak or no citations — [Wikipedia RX-11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamaha_RX-11). Wikipedia's article on Los Prisioneros' *Pateando piedras* says that album used a borrowed Linn drum machine — [Wikipedia: Pateando piedras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pateando_piedras)
- MR-10: "almost totally untapped by producers" — [Samples From Mars](https://samplesfrommars.com/products/mr10-from-mars)

### Inferences
- The "You Spin Me Round" RX5 claim looks doubtful, because the RX5 dates from 1986–87 (above) and that single is from 1984–85 (single date from general knowledge, not checked here).

### Gaps
- No verified recordings found for the RX21, RX21L, MR-10 or TG33. No published step charts or transcriptions found for *Floodland* tracks, *Brotherhood* tracks or Erasure songs. More searching was blocked by the exhausted search budget.
