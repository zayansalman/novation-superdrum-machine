# Boss Dr. Rhythm drum machines: hardware setup, preset patterns and recordings (DR-55, DR-110, DR-202, DR-220A/E, DR-550, DR-660)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Primary sources: the official Boss/Roland owner's manuals for the DR-550, DR-550MKII, DR-660 and DR-202 (scanned PDFs on Roland's CDN, read page by page), and the DR-110 owner's manual (a scan hosted on a fan site). No DR-55 or DR-220A/E owner's manual text could be retrieved: ManualsLib and manualmachine block automated fetches, and polynominal hosts the DR-55 manual only as a .zip. Those two machines therefore rest on secondary sources. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so recordings for the DR-220, DR-550 and DR-202, step charts for famous grooves, and free MIDI files could not be searched for (see Gaps under questions 8 and 9). Pack folders were inspected read-only with `ls -R` and `afinfo` on 2026-11-13.

## 1. Voices in front-panel or owner's-manual order, with exact labels

### Takeaway
All seven voice sets are documented. The DR-110, DR-550, DR-660 and DR-202 labels come straight from the manuals. The DR-55 and DR-220A/E labels come from secondary sources, so their exact printed panel legends are unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55 (analog, 4 voices):** bass drum, snare drum, rim shot, hi-hat, abbreviated BD, SD, RS, HH. — [polynominal DR-55](https://www.polynominal.com/site/studio/gear/drum/boss-dr55/boss-dr-55.html); [MusicTech](https://www.musictech.com/features/opinion-analysis/boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
- DR-55: analog, 4-voice, "no toms, cymbals or claps"; one closed hat only. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
- DR-55 panel: Volume, Tempo, Tone and Accent-level knobs on the left. Hi-Hat, Pattern, Variation, Mode and Sound-Select switches, a rotary pattern selector, and Start/Stop buttons on the right. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/). polynominal counts "4 potentiometers, a rotary selector, 2 push push buttons and 4 switch tabs". — [polynominal DR-55](https://www.polynominal.com/site/studio/gear/drum/boss-dr55/boss-dr-55.html)
- **DR-110 (analog, 6 voices):** instrument keys left to right: ACCENT, BASS DRUM, SNARE DRUM, O.HIHAT, C.HIHAT, CYMBAL, HAND CLAP. Knobs: TEMPO (SLOW–FAST), BALANCE (5–0–5), ACCENT (0–10), VOLUME (0–10). — [DR-110 owner's manual, cover and p.9](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf)
- DR-110 LCD step-grid rows: C HIHAT, O HIHAT, Snare Drum, Bass Drum, AC/CY/HCP. Accent, cymbal and clap share one display row. — [DR-110 manual cover/p.9](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf)
- DR-110 has "6 Sound Sources", which can also be played in real time from the instrument keys. — [DR-110 manual p.3](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf); the six voices are bass drum, snare drum, open hi-hat, closed hi-hat, hand clap and cymbal. — [Wikipedia DR-110](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-110_Dr._Rhythm)
- **DR-220A (acoustic set):** LT (low tom), MT (medium tom), HT (high tom), CCY (crash cymbal), RIM (rim shot), HCP (handclap), CH (closed hi-hat), OH (open hi-hat), BD, SD, RCY (ride cymbal), on a four-by-three pad grid. — [Wikipedia DR-220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-220), citing the owner's manual '88-3-A3-19S
- **DR-220E (electronic set):** LT, MT, HT, CHY (China cymbal), CB (cowbell), SLP (vibraslap), CH, OH, BD, SD, CUP (cup cymbal). — [Wikipedia DR-220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-220)
- DR-220A/E: 12 pads, not velocity-sensitive; 12-bit PCM, 11 PCM waves in ROM. — [polynominal DR-220A](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-A/index.html)
- **DR-550 (Mk I):** 12 key pads with printed legends, 1–4: CLAP, COWBELL, CRASH, RIDE; 5–8: S STICK, L TOM, M TOM, H TOM; 9–12: KICK, SNARE, C HH, O HH. — [DR-550 owner's manual pp.1, 9](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- DR-550 factory Pad Bank A puts BELL (ride cymbal bell) on pad 4, even though the pad is printed RIDE. Bank B pad 4 is RIDE. — [DR-550 manual p.16](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- DR-550MKII keeps the same 12 printed pad legends. — [DR-550MKII owner's manual, cover](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550MKII_OM.pdf)
- **DR-660:** 16 key pads, 1–4: CRASH 1, CRASH 2, BELL, RIDE; 5–8: TOM 1, TOM 2, TOM 3, TOM 4; 9–12: SIDE STICK, CLAP, CLOSED HH, OPEN HH; 13–16: KICK, SNARE, PEDAL HH, COWBELL. — [DR-660 owner's manual pp.7, 17](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
- **DR-202:** 13 drum pads plus a BASS button. Lower row: BASS, KICK1 (C), SNARE1 (D), CLOSED HH (E), OPEN HH (F), RIDE (G), CRASH (A), PERC1 (B), PERC2 (C). Upper row: KICK2 (C#/Db), SNARE2 (D#/Eb), HIT1 (F#/Gb), HIT2 (G#/Ab), HIT3 (A#/Bb). — [DR-202 owner's manual cover, p.6](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
- DR-202 manual's own instrument order (mute screen): BASS, KICK 1, SNARE1, CLOSED HH, OPEN HH, RIDE, CRASH, PERC1, PERC2, KICK2, SNARE2, HIT1, HIT2, HIT3, EXT PART. — [DR-202 manual p.32](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
- DR-202: "Thirteen drum and percussion instruments are assigned to the drum parts" — [DR-202 manual p.24](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf); each kit has 13 percussion voices and one bass voice — [Sound On Sound, Dec 1998](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4924999)

### Inferences
- The DR-202 pads are laid out as a one-octave keyboard (KICK1 = C up to PERC2 = C). The physical left-to-right order is therefore KICK1, KICK2, SNARE1, SNARE2, CLOSED HH, OPEN HH, HIT1, RIDE, HIT2, CRASH, HIT3, PERC1, PERC2. HIT1–3 sit on General MIDI tom notes (50, 47, 43), so in many kits they probably hold toms or hits.
- DR-220: read row by row, the Wikipedia order would give LT MT HT CCY / RIM HCP CH OH / BD SD RCY, plus a 12th pad that is not a sound. This is unverified.
- DR-55 "Sound Select" probably chooses which voice (BD, SD, RS or accent) is being written in step-write mode. This is inferred from the step-write descriptions; see question 3.
- For a faithful Drum Rack, use the front-panel order above. The DR-110 ACCENT key and the DR-202 BASS button are not drum sounds.

### Gaps
- DR-55 exact printed legends and the DR-220 physical pad positions, including what the DR-220's 12th pad does: the owner's manuals were not retrievable.

## 2. Sounds that share one voice or cut each other off

### Takeaway
The DR-110 closed hat chokes its open hat. The DR-550 and DR-660 have programmable exclusive groups. The DR-550's factory banks put the hat pairs in EXC1/EXC2. The DR-220A/E squeeze 11 sounds into 6 shared channels. The DR-55 has a single hat. For the DR-202, choke behaviour was not found in the manual pages read.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55:** only one closed hat; no open hat. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
- **DR-110:** both hats are built from the "High Metal" signal of the cymbal generator. The closed hat "can also shut off the decay of the open hat". The cymbal uses High Metal for its attack and Low Metal for its tail. — [Electric Druid DR-110 analysis](https://electricdruid.net/?p=71361)
- DR-110 manual charts mark some steps "Closed & Open Hihat", meaning both are written on the same step. — [DR-110 manual p.36](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf)
- **DR-220A/E:** 6-voice polyphony, and "some sounds share channels"; rimshot and clap cannot play at the same time. — [polynominal DR-220A](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-A/index.html). Because of "channel sharing", only one tom can be used per beat. — [Wikipedia DR-220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-220)
- polynominal groups the DR-220A sounds as kick, snare, hihat, rimshot/clap, hi/low/mid tom, crash/ride cymbal, and the DR-220E sounds as electro BD, electro SN, hihat, cowbell/slap, hi/low/mid tom, china cymbal/cup. — [polynominal DR-220A](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-A/index.html); [polynominal DR-220E](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-E/index.html)
- **DR-550:** each pad has an Assign Type: MONO, POLY, EXC1 or EXC2. Sounds with the same EXC number "will not be layered", "useful for such sounds as the open and closed hi-hats". MONO cuts off the pad's own previous note. — [DR-550 manual pp.56–57](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- DR-550 factory banks: CHH1/OHH1 are EXC1 on pads 11–12 of banks A and B; CHH2/OHH2 are EXC2 on pads 11–12 of bank D. — [DR-550 manual pp.16–17](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- DR-550MKII factory banks: CHH1/OHH1 EXC1 (bank A), PHH1/CHH2/OHH2 EXC1 (bank B pads 5, 11, 12), CHH3/OHH3 EXC2 (bank D). — [DR-550MKII manual pp.16–17](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550MKII_OM.pdf)
- **DR-660:** A.TYPE per pad: POLY, POLY EXC1–7, MONO, MONO EXC1–7. "Open and Closed Hi-hat ... can be set to the same EXC numbers"; MONO EXC is suggested for pairs such as Guiro Short and Guiro Long. — [DR-660 manual pp.69–70](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
- DR-660 polyphony: 12 voices. — [DR-660 manual p.121](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
- **DR-202:** 24-voice polyphony; some instruments use two voices each (Voice column of the instrument list). — [DR-202 manual pp.25, 70](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)

### Inferences
- Suggested Drum Rack choke groups:
  - DR-110: C.HIHAT + O.HIHAT. Documented.
  - DR-550: C HH + O HH. Documented. For bank-D sounds use a second group.
  - DR-660: CLOSED HH + OPEN HH + PEDAL HH. The mechanism is documented, but the factory A.TYPE settings are not printed in the factory-kit table.
  - DR-220A: [LT MT HT], [RIM HCP], [CH OH], [CCY RCY]. The tom and rim/clap groups are documented. The hat and cymbal pairs are inferred from polynominal's six groupings matching the 6-voice polyphony.
  - DR-220E: [LT MT HT], [CB SLP], [CH OH], [CHY CUP]. Same basis.
- DR-202: a hat choke is likely but unconfirmed. Leave it as an optional choke.

### Gaps
- DR-660 factory A.TYPE (EXC) values per pad; DR-202 hat choke behaviour; whether the DR-55 rim shot and snare share circuitry.

## 3. Per-voice controls, accent, flam, shuffle and tuning

### Takeaway
The analog machines have almost no per-voice control. The DR-55 has only global knobs. The DR-110 has a global BALANCE and ACCENT, and its accent is global per step. The DR-220 has 5-level accent and volume per voice. The ROM machines have deep per-pad editing: the DR-550 has level, tone colour, decay, assign type, accent follow and pan. The DR-660 adds pitch, nuance, velocity curves, flam and roll. The DR-202 has level, pitch, pan, effect sends, filter cutoff/resonance and decay, plus rolls and groove quantise.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55:**
  - Accent is programmable and adjustable in amount; Tone sets the overall sound from dark to bright and "affects the mix only"; there is no balance control. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
  - Conflict: MusicTech calls Tone "a simple filter-based control that tames the hi-hat's sharpness". — [MusicTech](https://www.musictech.com/features/opinion-analysis/boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
  - ACCENT "emphasizes all sounds present in a step". Hats are not programmable per step and play at 8ths, 12ths or 16ths, or off. — [polynominal DR-55](https://www.polynominal.com/site/studio/gear/drum/boss-dr55/boss-dr-55.html). Bonedo lists the hat switch as off/8ths/16ths and says there is no shuffle. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
  - Memory: six 16-step patterns plus two 12-step patterns (7 and 8, for 3/4 or 6/8). Variation A/B gives up to 32 steps. No song chaining. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
  - Only kick, snare, rim shot and accent can be placed in a pattern in step mode. — Vintage Synth Explorer, as quoted in a search-result excerpt; the page was not fetched. [Vintage Synth DR-55](https://www.vintagesynth.com/boss/dr-55-dr-rhythm)
  - Jacks: DBS sync out (one pulse per step), CSQ out (one pulse per accented step), footswitch start/stop, mono out; no sync input. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/); [polynominal DR-55](https://www.polynominal.com/site/studio/gear/drum/boss-dr55/boss-dr-55.html)
- **DR-110:**
  - Accent is added per step, and "the amount of accent is globally variable with a knob". BALANCE varies bass and snare against hi-hat and cymbal. Tempo is 45–300 BPM. No tonal variation of the voices is possible. — [Wikipedia DR-110](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-110_Dr._Rhythm)
  - The accent trigger creates "a short decay envelope which controls the overall output level" through a VCA, so it lifts the whole mix on that step. No individual outputs, no panning. — [Electric Druid](https://electricdruid.net/?p=71361)
  - 12/16-step setting for triplets ("It is possible to write a triplet or triple time rhythm"). STEP WRITE and TAP WRITE modes. ACC TRIG OUT jack "for controlling a synthesizer". — [DR-110 manual pp.3, 9](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf)
- **DR-220A/E:**
  - Each voice has Accent and Volume, each 0–5. Tempo 40–250 BPM. Patterns up to 16 steps (12 in 3/4). Tuning is not mentioned. — [Wikipedia DR-220](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boss_DR-220)
  - "Accent (5 levels) and Mix volume (5 levels)"; no per-instrument tuning mentioned. — [polynominal DR-220A](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-A/index.html)
- **DR-550:**
  - Per pad: Assign (any of the 48 sounds; a sound may sit on several pads), Tone Color 0–7, Decay −32 to +32, Assign Type MONO/POLY/EXC1,2, Accent Follow −7 to +7, Pan L3–L1, C, R1–R3 (7 positions), Level 0–15. — [DR-550 manual pp.54–60, 99](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
  - Accent: the ACC button is held while writing. Accent Follow is added to Level. Using several pads with the same sound gives "up to a maximum of 22 levels of accents". — [DR-550 manual pp.58, 65](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
  - Factory "Tone Color and Decay are set at '0', and Accent Follow is set to '7'". — [p.15](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
  - Scale options: 32nd note, 16th-note triplet, 16th note, 8th-note triplet. Last Step 1–16 sets the pattern length. Minimum timing resolution is a 32nd note. Tempo 40–250. 12 voices. — [DR-550 manual pp.61, 99](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf) (scale values read from the manual's diagrams)
  - No flam or shuffle function appears in the manual's contents or index. — [DR-550 manual pp.3–5, 101–102](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- **DR-550MKII:** same pad parameters; 12 non-dynamic pads; patterns of at most 16 steps at 1/32 resolution; no fill patterns. — [Music Technology, Oct 1992](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/boss-dr550-mkii-and-dr660-drum-machines/1176)
- **DR-660:**
  - Per pad: ASSIGN, LEVEL 0–15, PITCH −2400 to +2400 in 10-cent units ("adjustable pitch range will vary from sound to sound"), DECAY −31 to +31, NUANCE −7 to +7 (only sounds marked * or **), PAN L7–C–R7/INDIV, A.TYPE. — [DR-660 manual pp.64–70](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
  - CRV velocity curve EXP1/LIN1/EXP2/LIN2/XFDO/XFDI/FIX1/FIX2, set per pad bank and not per pad. — [p.71](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
  - 16 velocity-sensitive pads that also respond to aftertouch, which controls roll volume. Reverb and chorus depth can be set per key pad. — [p.9](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
  - ROLL and FLAM buttons; roll speed, flam interval and flam balance are editable. — [pp.19, 38](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf). Swing and Timing Shift. — [contents p.4](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf). 96 ppqn; tempo 20–260. — [p.121](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
- **DR-202:**
  - Per instrument: LEVEL 0–15, PITCH −100 to +100, PAN Left 7–Center–Right 7, REV/DLY SEND 0–100, FLANGER SEND 0–100, CUTOFF −50 to +50, RESONANCE −50 to +50, DECAY −50 to +50. — [DR-202 manual p.68](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
  - The Realtime Modify knobs (CUTOFF, RESONANCE, DECAY) act on the instrument picked with the INSTRUMENT SELECT knob, or on ALL DRUMS / ALL INST. — [p.29](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
  - Accent: each kit has an accent setting of 1–8 ("Changing the Accent (Volume)"). — [p.33](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf). Notes carry ACC 1–8, Gate Time 1–2047 and Time Shift −12 to +12. — [p.69](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
  - The pads are not velocity-sensitive. — [SOS Dec 1998](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4924999)
  - Roll function with 68 types (00–67, including Flam 1–3 and Fill 1–8) and speed 0–127. Groove Quantize with 20 templates. Quantize down to a 32nd-note triplet. Portamento and octave for bass. Mute and solo per instrument. — [pp.33–34, 68, 78–79](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)

### Inferences
- For the DR-55 and DR-110, accent is a whole-step volume boost and not per voice. In MIDI, raise velocity on every note at an accented step.
- DR-220 accent is set per voice (0–5) but entered per step. How it is entered is unknown.

### Gaps
- How DR-220 accents are entered; the DR-55's exact Mode/Pattern switch positions; whether the DR-55 hat really has a 12ths position (polynominal says yes, Bonedo lists only 8ths and 16ths).

## 4. Built-in effects

### Takeaway
Only the DR-660 (reverb plus chorus/flanger, with per-pad sends and dry individual outputs) and the DR-202 (reverb/delay plus flanger, with per-instrument sends, plus per-instrument filters) have effects. The DR-55, DR-110, DR-220A/E and DR-550/MKII have none.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55, DR-110, DR-220A/E:** no effects are listed. DR-55 controls are Volume, Tempo, Tone and Accent. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/). DR-110 features list no effects. — [DR-110 manual p.3](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf). DR-220 controls are an LCD, 16 buttons, 12 pads and a volume slider. — [polynominal DR-220A](http://www.polynominal.com/Boss-Dr-220-A/index.html)
- **DR-550:** the specifications list sounds, pad parameters, patterns, songs, cassette storage, resolution, tempo and controls, with no effect. — [DR-550 manual pp.99–100](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf)
- **DR-660:** "two built-in effects, Reverb and Chorus".
  - Reverb types HALL, ROOM, PLATE, DELAY, PAN-DLY; R.TIME 0–31; R.LPF (reverb pre low-pass filter) 0–7; D.FDBK 0–15 for DELAY/PAN-DLY.
  - Chorus C.TYPE CHORUS or FLANGER; C.DPTH 0–15; C.RATE 0–15.
  - "The depth ... of Reverb and Chorus can be set for the entire Drum Kit, or for each sound within the Drum Kit". The INDIVIDUAL 1/2 outputs are dry.
  - — [DR-660 manual pp.24–25, 76–77](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-660_OM.pdf)
- DR-660: reverb cannot run alongside Out 1, and chorus cannot run alongside Out 2. — [Music Technology, Oct 1992](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/boss-dr550-mkii-and-dr660-drum-machines/1176)
- **DR-202:** "two kinds of internal effects systems: reverb/delay and flanger".
  - REVERB/DELAY TYPE: Small Room, Studio, Club, Lounge, Large Hall, Dark Hall, Plate 1, Plate 2, Stereo Delay, Pan Delay. Reverb time 0–100; reverb level 0–127. Delay time 5–450 ms or tempo-related note values (not synced to external MIDI clock); delay level 0–100; delay feedback 0–127.
  - FLANGER TYPE: Jet, Soft, Hard, Cold; rate 0–100; level 0–127.
  - Each instrument has REV/DLY SEND and FLANGER SEND (0–100), so the effects apply to the drums and the bass.
  - — [DR-202 manual pp.30–31, 68](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)
- DR-202: "14 digital filters (13 percussion, one bass)" with Cutoff, Resonance and Decay; "eight reverb types and two delay types". — [SOS Dec 1998](https://www.soundonsound.com/node/4924999). The front panel also has a LOW knob marked CUT/BOOST. — [DR-202 manual pp.1, 6](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-202_OM.pdf)

### Inferences
- Sampled DR-660 and DR-202 one-shots were probably recorded dry. Use Drum Rack return chains to recreate the per-pad sends: Reverb plus Chorus/Flanger for the DR-660; Reverb or Delay plus Flanger, and per-pad Auto Filter for cutoff and resonance, for the DR-202.

### Gaps
- What the DR-202 LOW knob does in detail (manual p.16 not read).

## 5. ROM sound banks, factory kits and MIDI note maps (DR-550, DR-660, DR-202)

### Takeaway
All three ROM lists were copied from the official manuals:
- DR-550: 48 sounds (MKII: 91), in four 12-pad banks with a factory note map.
- DR-660: 255 numbered sounds (0–254, plus 255 = OFF) in kits of 55 notes (MIDI 27–81), General MIDI-style.
- DR-202: 256 instruments, 49 bass (00–48) and 207 drum (49–255), on 13 pads at fixed notes 35–61, with rolls on 100–112.

### Cited Findings
**DR-550 (Mk I): "The following 48 sounds are contained in the DR-550"** — [DR-550 manual pp.54–55](http://cdn.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/DR-550_OM.pdf). Ordered list, display code and name. The unit uses display codes; the positions 1–48 below are list order, not hardware numbers.
- Kick (1–5): KCK1 Room kick, KCK2 Dry kick, KCK3 Solid kick, KCK4 Face kick, KCK5 Techno kick
- Snare (6–11): SNR1 Reverb snare, SNR2 Real snare, SNR3 Rim shot snare, SNR4 Birch snare, SNR5 808 snare, SNR6 Bright snare
- Side stick (12): SIDE Side stick
- Tom (13–20): LTM1 Low room tom, MTM1 Mid room tom, HTM1 High room tom, LTM2 Low attack tom, MTM2 Mid attack tom, HTM2 High attack tom, LTM3 Low electronic tom, HTM3 High electronic tom
- Hi-hat (21–25): CHH1 Closed hi-hat, OHH1 Open hi-hat, PHH1 Pedal closed hi-hat, CHH2 808 closed hi-hat, OHH2 808 open hi-hat
- Cymbal (26–28): CRSH Crash cymbal, RIDE Ride cymbal, BELL Ride cymbal bell
- Percussion (29–45): CLAP 808 hand clap, COW1 Cowbell, COW2 808 cowbell, CLVS Claves, CG_L Open low conga, CG_S Slap high conga, CG_H Mute high conga, BG_L Low bongo, BG_H High bongo, TB_L Low timbale, TB_H High timbale, AG_L Low agogo, AG_H High agogo, SHKr Shaker, CABA Cabasa, TMBr Tambourine, WHSL Whistle

- DR-660 notes follow the General MIDI drum map (36 kick, 38 snare, 42/44/46 hats, 49/57 crashes, 51 ride, 53 bell). A Drum Rack can use those notes directly.
- DR-550 note 0 is a placeholder: per the duplicate-note rule only bank B pad 3 (CRSH) would respond to note 0, so treat the other note-0 pads as unmapped.

## 6. What each pack folder contains

### Takeaway
All ten folders hold only WAV one-shots, with no loops, MIDI or documentation, and the longest file is 4.4 s. Every file's duration was checked; the sample format (44.1 kHz/16-bit) was checked on one file per folder. The analog and DR-220 folders use descriptive names. The DR-550 folder uses names. The DR-660 and DR-202 folders use category + number names that can be lined up with the ROM lists only by inference.

### Inferences
- **DR-55:** the subfolder-free "Boss DR-55" set (9 snares, 2 kicks, 2 hats) probably captures different Tone and Accent settings of the 4 voices. What "DR-55Pop" is remains unknown. The "DR55" files are tiny (<0.1 s) and may be truncated.
- **DR-110:** "Crash" and "Ride" in "Boss DR-110" are most likely two treatments of the single CYMBAL voice, since the DR-110 has no separate crash/ride. "dR110" is exactly the six voices.
- **DR-220:** the "Boss DR-220" set matches the DR-220A voices if "Clave" is the RIM voice. "DR-220a" maps one-to-one onto the A voices. In "DR-220e", "Slap" = SLP (vibraslap), and "Crash"/"Cymbal" probably = CHY (China) and CUP (cup); that pairing is unverified.
- **DR-550:** the subfolders line up with the Mk I list of 48: 5 kicks, 6 snares, a side stick (labelled "Rim Shot"), 8 toms (3 high, 2 mid, 3 low = HTM1–3, MTM1–2, LTM1–3), 2 hat pairs, crash, ride and bell (Ride-01/02), and most of the percussion and effects. PHH1 (pedal hat) is missing, and possibly SHKr.
- **DR-550 top-level set:** Maracas, Clap02 and a third hat pair exist only on the MKII (MARA, CLP2, CHH3/OHH3). A splash cymbal is on neither list. So the top-level set may come from a MKII, or be loosely labelled; its origin is unclear.
- **DR-202:** the pack's prefixes match the inferred ROM ranges closely: kik 31 = 31; cla 13 = 13; rim 6 = 6; cym 10 = 10; per 63 = 63. snr 48 vs 49, tom 15 vs 14 and hat 22 vs 21 differ by one each (208 files vs 207 ROM drums). File N is probably ROM instrument (range start + N − 1), but this is unverified.
- **DR-660:** Snare01–77 probably = ROM 51–127 (72 snares + 5 side sticks). Hat_C01–06 = the 6 closed/pedal hats (PopCHH, RealCH, RealPH, BrsCHH, 808CHH, 78 CHH). Hat_O = the 5 open hats. Crash01–03 + Ride01–03 + SplashCym = the 7 cymbals. Kick01–50 vs 51 ROM kicks. Perc01–72 vs 70 ROM entries 185–254. All 39 toms are absent. All of this is inferred.

### Gaps
- Exact file-to-ROM-number mapping for the DR-660 and DR-202 packs. This needs listening or metadata, which was out of scope (no playback allowed).

## 7. Factory preset patterns and published step grids

### Takeaway
- The DR-110 manual prints its 16 presets (C-1 to D-8, unnamed) in staff notation, and a "Sample Rhythm" section with exact step grids. Eight of those grids are copied below.
- The DR-660 (100 presets) and DR-202 (400 presets) manuals list names only.
- The DR-550's 64 presets were in a separate "Preset Pattern Scores" booklet that was not found.
- The DR-55 has no factory presets documented. The DR-220's 32 preset names were not found.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55:** memory is 6 × 16-step and 2 × 12-step user patterns, with no song chaining. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/). No source mentions factory presets.
- **DR-110:**
  - "16 different Rhythm Patterns are preprogrammed". — [DR-110 manual p.10](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf). They are in banks C and D, with 16 user patterns in A and B. — [Bonedo DR-110](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-110)
  - The manual shows presets C-1 to C-8 and D-1 to D-8 as staff notation (rows C.HIHAT/O.HIHAT, SNARE DRUM, BASS DRUM, CYMBAL, HAND CLAP) with no style names. Notation key: plain note = closed hat, x-note = open hat, circled-x note = "Closed & Open Hihat". — [DR-110 manual pp.36–39](https://www.theninhotline.com/dr110/Boss-dr110-manual.pdf)

## 8. Famous recordings that used each machine

### Takeaway
Evidence is decent for the DR-55 (New Order, The Cure, Joy Division, Cybotron) and the DR-110 (They Might Be Giants' early demos), but mostly second-hand. It is thin for the DR-660 (Squarepusher, Memphis rap), from gear databases. No recordings were found for the DR-220, DR-550 or DR-202, because the search budget ran out before they could be searched. No published step chart of any of these grooves was found.

### Cited Findings
- **DR-55 – New Order, "Truth" (Movement, 1981):** the band "featured the percussion sounds prominently" on "Truth". This cites a 2016 Electricity Club interview with Stephen Morris, which was not retrieved; the Electricity Club URL returned 404. — [Microchop](https://microchop.substack.com/p/the-boss-dr-55-dr-rhythm-drum-machine)
- **DR-55 – The Cure, "One Hundred Years" (Pornography, 1982):** "The Cure used it for their classic '100 Years'". This is Bonedo's claim, with no primary quote. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
- **DR-55 – Joy Division, "Isolation" (Closer, 1980):** Bonedo says Joy Division used it "for the song 'Isolation'" and lists "She's Lost Control" only in passing. — [Bonedo DR-55](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/vintage-drum-machine-boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/). Conflict: a search-result summary reported Stephen Morris saying he got the DR-55 just after Closer; the source page was not retrievable. — [Equipboard: Stephen Morris](https://equipboard.com/pros/stephen-morris)
- **DR-55 – Cybotron, "Clear" (1983):** Juan Atkins used the DR-55 in production, per his 2012 MusicRadar interview. The interview itself was not retrieved. — [Microchop](https://microchop.substack.com/p/the-boss-dr-55-dr-rhythm-drum-machine)
- **DR-55 – Depeche Mode:** it was "the first machine they used live", with Dave Gahan choosing among eight patterns on stage. This is the author's account, and no recording is named. — [MusicTech](https://www.musictech.com/features/opinion-analysis/boss-dr-rhythm-dr-55/)
- **DR-110 – They Might Be Giants:** the DR-110 was the band's first drum machine, bought "in late 1983 or 1984". It is heard on "1999", "Confusing The Mind", "I Need Some Lovin'", "Next Plane To London", the first demo of "Don't Let's Start" and songs on the 1984 Promotional Demo Tape. The wiki cites a Flickr photo and John Flansburgh's 2009 Magnet piece. — [This Might Be A Wiki](https://tmbw.net/wiki/Drum_Machine)
- **DR-660 – Squarepusher, Feed Me Weird Things (1996):** Equipboard lists the DR-660 and quotes Tom Jenkinson saying he "made my first records using the Boss DR660". Seen only in a search excerpt; the page returned 403. — [Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/albums/squarepusher-feed-me-weird-things-remastered)
- **DR-660 – Shawty Pimp, Volume 1 (1994):** Equipboard lists the DR-660; the excerpt says the 1993 "Solo Tape" used it exclusively. Search excerpt only. — [Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/albums/shawty-pimp-volume-1)
- **DR-660 – Dance Mania ghetto house:** a search excerpt claimed the DR-660 was "the main production tool for most Dance Mania ghetto house tracks". The underlying page could not be identified or fetched, so this is unverified.

### Inferences
- New Order "Truth" is the best-supported DR-55 recording, because it rests on a named drummer interview. The Cure and Joy Division claims need a primary source. Joy Division "Isolation" is contradicted on timing.

### Gaps
- No step chart or transcription of any of these grooves was found.
- No recordings were found for the DR-220A/E, DR-550/MKII or DR-202, and no further DR-110 and DR-660 credits. The search budget was exhausted before these searches could run.
